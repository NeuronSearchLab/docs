title: 'Context groups'

description: 'Apply rules across the rails a page shows together, such as never repeating an item between them.'
---

A page often shows several rails at once: "For you", "Similar items", "Trending near you". Each rail is its own [context](/console/contexts), ranked independently, so the same item can turn up in two of them. A **context group** adds rules that apply across the rails of one page.

Group rules apply when a page asks for the group's rails together in one request: the [batch endpoint](/integrate/recommendations#several-rails-on-one-page), the [embed](/embed), or an SDK's `getRecommendationSets()`. Rails requested one at a time are not affected.

## Create a group

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Contexts">
    Go to **Relevance → Contexts** and select **New group**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Name it after the page, such as "Homepage", and add an optional description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add rails">
    Add contexts from the list, or drag them onto the group in the contexts table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order the rails">
    Drag rails into priority order. The rail at the top claims items first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the rules">
    Turn on the rules below and save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Group rules

| Rule | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Don't repeat items across rails** | An item appears in one rail at most. The highest rail that ranked it keeps it. |
| **Keep rails full** | When group rules remove items from a rail, the rail takes the next ones from its own ranking, to try to fill its limit; it can still return fewer items when there are not enough eligible candidates. |
| **Limit per attribute across the group** | Caps how many items share a metadata value across all the group's rails, for example at most 3 items per `author`. |

## Exempt rails

Mark a rail as **exempt** when it should ignore the group rules, such as a "Recently viewed" rail that is meant to repeat items. An exempt rail may repeat items, and the items it shows do not count against the other rails.

## Removing a group

Select **Delete group** in the group's settings. Its contexts stay as they are, in no group.
