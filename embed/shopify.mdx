The NeuronSearchLab app for Shopify adds personalised product recommendations
to your storefront without code. Shopify already knows which product a page
shows, what is in the basket, who is signed in and what was bought. The app
reads that data and passes it to NeuronSearchLab, which keeps your catalogue
in sync and serves each shopper products they are likely to want.

Everything is set up from inside Shopify admin. You do not need an existing
NeuronSearchLab account, and there are no keys to copy.

<Note>
  The app covers the manual steps described on the main embed page. With it installed you do not need [page context](/embed#page-context), [category and search](/embed#category-and-search-pages) or [conversions](/embed#conversions).
</Note>

<Info>
  The app has been submitted to the Shopify App Store and is in Shopify's review. It can be installed once Shopify publishes the listing; email [support@neuronsearchlab.com](mailto:support@neuronsearchlab.com) to be told when it is available.
</Info>

## What you need

- A Shopify store with the Online Store sales channel and an Online Store 2.0
  theme (any theme from the Shopify Theme Store qualifies).
- Permission to install apps on the store.

## Install and set up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    Install **NeuronSearchLab** from the Shopify App Store. Shopify asks you
    to approve the access the app needs; see [Permissions](#permissions).
    The app then opens in Shopify admin, under **Apps → NeuronSearchLab**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Select **View plans** and choose a plan. Billing is through Shopify, on
    your Shopify invoice. Once the plan is active, the app finishes setup
    automatically:

    - creates a NeuronSearchLab workspace for your store;
    - starts syncing your active products;
    - creates the storefront key and stores it where your theme can read it;
    - connects a checkout pixel under **Settings → Customer events**.

    The first catalogue sync usually takes a few minutes. The **Your
    catalogue** card shows its progress and the number of products synced.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the app embed">
    Select **Turn on app embed**. Your theme editor opens with the
    **NeuronSearchLab** app embed switched on; select **Save**. The embed
    loads the recommendation widget and reports page, product, basket and
    signed-in customer context on every page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place recommendation strips">
    Select **Add to product pages** or **Add to home page**. The theme editor
    opens with a **Recommendations** block added; position it and select
    **Save**. On a product page, **Related to this page** shows similar
    products. **Personalised to the visitor** shows a feed for the person
    browsing. You can add the block to any other template from the theme
    editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it is live">
    Visit a product page on your storefront. The strip shows products from
    your catalogue. Back in the app, the app embed card changes to **Live**
    once the storefront has requested recommendations. To see what the widget
    is doing on any page, add `?nsl_debug=1` to the page URL.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Plans

Plans are billed by Shopify, in US dollars, every 30 days. You can change or
cancel your plan at any time from **Change plan** in the app.

| Plan | Price | Storefront recommendation requests per month |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $199 / month | 100,000 |
| Growth | $449 / month | 250,000 |

Shopify shows any free trial when you choose a plan. There are no usage
charges on top of the plan price: a store that goes over its monthly
requests keeps showing recommendations and is not charged extra. A store that
regularly needs more should move to a larger plan.

## Permissions

| Access | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Read products and inventory | Sync your catalogue, and leave out products that cannot be bought |
| Read orders | Learn from purchases, so recommendations improve faster |
| Write pixels and read customer events | Connect the checkout pixel that reports completed orders |

## Catalogue sync

The app syncs products that are **active, published to the Online Store and
in stock** (at least one variant for sale), including on password-protected
and development stores. It reads each product's title, description, vendor,
type, tags, price, SKU, image and storefront URL. A product that is
unpublished, sells out or is deleted stops being recommended at the next
sync, and returns when it is available again.

Products sync again whenever one is created, updated or deleted, and at
least every six hours. Select **Sync now** in the app to start one
straight away.

## What the storefront sends

Each value comes from Shopify's own objects, so there is no tagging to write
and nothing to keep correct as your theme changes.

| Shopify | Becomes |
| --- | --- |
| `product.selected_or_first_available_variant.sku` | the item this page is about |
| `collection.handle` | the category a listing page is showing |
| `search.terms` | the search a visitor just ran |
| `cart.items` | the basket, on every page |
| `customer.id` | who is signed in, hashed per workspace before anything is stored |
| `checkout_completed` | the sale |

## Conversions

Orders are reported by a web pixel rather than by theme code, because
Shopify's checkout is not part of your theme and no theme code runs there.
The app creates the pixel for you; you can see it under **Settings →
Customer events**.

Orders arrive as the `order_reported` signal, bound by default to a
zero-weight event, because a browser cannot prove a sale took place. They are
used for attribution and revenue reporting. Product views and basket
additions come from the app embed as `view` and `add_to_cart`; see
[Events](/embed#events). Verified orders read with the app's order access use
the same order ID, so a verified order replaces the browser's report of it
rather than counting twice.

## Placements and the console

The app covers setup. To configure what a strip serves, view analytics or add
ranking rules, select **Open NeuronSearchLab console** in the app's
**Placements and analytics** section.
It signs you in to your store's workspace with your Shopify account, without
a separate password.

A strip decides where recommendations appear. What it serves, how many items
it shows and how each card reads are set by the placement in the console, so
those change without a theme edit. Give the block a **Placement name** in the
theme editor, then create a placement with the same name in the console. A
placement the console does not recognise still renders with your workspace
defaults, so renaming one does not blank a live page.

## Privacy and data

Where your shop uses Shopify's customer privacy API and a visitor has not
allowed analytics, the widget continues to render recommendations but stops
describing the visitor: cart and customer are withheld.

The storefront sends the customer **id**, never a name, email or address. It
is hashed with a per-workspace key before storage. Order history is read
through Shopify's Admin API and the customer ID, or a guest's email, is
hashed the same way before anything is stored.

NeuronSearchLab handles Shopify's privacy requests automatically. A customer
redaction request deletes that customer's events and profile. When you
uninstall, the storefront key stops working at once and the pixel is
disconnected; 48 hours later, when Shopify sends its shop redaction request,
the store's catalogue, events and keys are deleted.

## Uninstalling

Uninstall from **Settings → Apps** in Shopify admin. This cancels your plan
and stops recommendations immediately; the app embed then renders nothing.
If you reinstall within 48 hours, your previous workspace and catalogue
are kept.

## Stores set up before the app

A store that previously connected to NeuronSearchLab with an Admin API token
in **Integrations → Shopify** keeps working. Its app embed can still use a
key typed into the embed's **Embed key** setting, which takes precedence over
the key the app provides.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause |
| --- | --- |
| The app asks you to choose a plan | No active plan; choose one to finish setup |
| No strip renders | The app embed is off, or the theme was not saved after turning it on |
| The strip is empty on a new store | The first catalogue sync has not finished; check **Your catalogue** in the app |
| Strip renders on some domains only | Open the app once after adding a domain; it adds your primary domain to the storefront key |
| Strip looks unrelated to the product | Add `?nsl_debug=1` to the page; the overlay shows whether the product was found in your catalogue |
| Conversions not appearing | Check the NeuronSearchLab pixel is connected under **Settings → Customer events**, and that the shopper allowed analytics |
